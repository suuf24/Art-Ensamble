Let's Learn Music!

We use music to express our feelings.

Music makes us happy! Music makes us sad!

Music Elements

Every song has three main elements.

♪

Notes (Nada)

A note is a sound. Notes make a pattern.

Example: Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Si, Do

♫

Rhythm (Irama)

Rhythm is a repeating pattern of sounds.

Example: Clap your hands! Clap, clap, clap!

♬

Melody (Melodi)

Melody is a line of high and low notes.

Example: We sing the melody of a song.

Types of Notes

There are three types of notes in songs.

1. Diatonic Notes

Diatonic notes are common in many songs. They use the scale: Do-Re-Mi-Fa-Sol-La-Si-Do.

Major notes sound happy and bright!

Minor notes sound sad and serious.

Play the Notes!

Click a key to play!

2. Pelog Notes

Pelog notes come from Indonesia. They sound unique and beautiful.

Notes: Do - Mi - Fa - Sol - Si

We use these notes in traditional songs.

3. Slendro Notes

Slendro notes also come from Indonesia. They have 5 notes.

Notes: Do - Re - Mi - Sol - La

We use these notes in gamelan music.

Tempo and Dynamics

Music has speed and volume!

What is Tempo?

Tempo is the speed of music. It tells us how fast or slow we play.

🐢

Slow

The music plays slowly.

🚶

Medium

The music plays normally.

🐇

Fast

The music plays quickly!

What is Dynamics?

Dynamics is how loud or soft the music sounds.

🤫

Soft (Piano)

We play quietly.

📢

Loud (Forte)

We play strongly!

Dynamics show feelings! Soft music feels calm. Loud music feels exciting!

Beat is the counting in music. Each song has different beats.

2/4

2 beats per measure

Example: Helele Uala De Teang

3/4

3 beats per measure

Example: Burung Tantina

Musical Instruments

There are two types of instruments.

🎹

Melodic Instruments

Melodic instruments play notes and melodies.

  • ● Pianica (we blow and press)
  • ● Piano (we press keys)
  • ● Violin (we use a bow)
  • ● Recorder (we blow)
🥁

Rhythmic Instruments

Rhythmic instruments keep the beat.

  • ● Drum (we hit it)
  • ● Tambourine (we shake it)
  • ● Gong (we hit it)
  • ● Tambur (from Bali)

Make Your Own Music!

We can make music with simple things!

👏

Clapping

Clap your hands! It makes "pak pak pak" sound.

🫙

Bottle

Hit a bottle with a pencil! It makes a ringing sound.

🥣

Bowl

Hit a bowl! It makes a "dung dung dung" sound.

Music Ensemble

Ensemble means playing music together in a group. We use different instruments together.

Melodic Group

They play the melody. Example: piano, recorder, violin.

Rhythmic Group

They keep the beat. Example: drum, tambourine, gong.

Harmonic Group

They play chords. Example: guitar, keyboard.
